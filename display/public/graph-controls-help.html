Graph Controls:
  • Drag a node to move it. It will stay fixed at the new position.
  • Shift-click a node to unfix it and let it move freely again.
  • Ctrl-drag and release a node to let it snap back to its original position.
  • Click a node to view details. Hover to see a quick info card.
  • Click clusters to expand and view members.
  • Use the Zoom buttons or mouse wheel to pan/zoom the graph.
